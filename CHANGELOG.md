# Changelog

## 0.7.0 (unreleased): David's fourth notes
- New menu look after the Street-Spec logo: a chrome, italic NIGHTSHIFT with a pink 夜勤
  stamp, italic titles, dark glass panels with thin frames, a vignette, no window title bars.
  The pixel fonts stay. Japanese text only on screen titles and the world's signs.
- Drive is now Track, Car, then a new Race settings screen (weather and time, shown on the
  track behind as you pick them), then Race.
- Loading screens: a spinning wheel at start-up; into a race, a still of the track, the wheel
  bottom left and a tip.
- Garage: a rail of tabs with pop-ups, the car fills the screen, drag to look round it,
  scroll or pinch to zoom, the camera stands further back. No fade on every choice. Ride
  height settles with a bounce. The Engine tab lifts the bonnet: each car has an engine
  bay showing the fitted intake, manifolds, ECU and turbos or supercharger, and a new part
  drops in when you fit it.
- The Nissani Skyliner (R34) is hidden until its body is rebuilt; the GT-Ar is the default car.
- Driving: the car bounces off walls instead of grinding along them; the camera jolts on
  gear changes; a minimap top right.
- Touch: pedals and a handbrake lever drawn as pictures; camera and pause moved top left;
  a double tap no longer zooms the page in and sticks.
- Sound: the crickets are gone; menu sounds that differ by action; backfires (crack, bang,
  echo), a gearbox clack with a bark on upshifts and a blip on downshifts, exhaust rasp, a
  rev limiter that bounces.
- New sky: gradient, sun or moon glow, clouds lit pink and gold at sunset, peach at dawn and
  by the city at night, stars. High graphics adds finer clouds, glow round bright lights
  and more real lamp lights.
- Particles: soft, turning smoke puffs and flame glows (PS2 style); sparks are streaks.
- Cockpit: grey plastics faintly lit, white backlit dials, a radio display, A-pillars.

## 0.6.0 (unreleased): the world
- New track: Parabolica Street, a Tokyo ring road round a park, shaped like Monza at about
  60% scale (3.6 km). Shops with lit fronts and neon signs, blocks of flats, vending machines,
  convenience stores, power lines, white LED lamps, cherry blossom in the park, footbridges
  and an elevated expressway crossing over.
- Time of day: Sunset, Night or Dawn, picked in the Time box on the car pick.
- Snow: a fourth weather with snow falling and lying on the verges and roofs; the least grip.
- Weather patches: fixed stretches of each track where the rain turns to a downpour, the fog
  thickens or the snow comes in harder (grip eases down a little, never snaps).
- The asset library (`?library`): every prop, one at a time, under the game's own light.

## 0.5.0 (unreleased): garage mods
- The Garage has eight tabs: Paint (colour and finish: gloss, metallic, pearl, matte), Decals
  (stripes, side stripe, race number, 走り屋 sticker), Wheels (six rim styles, rim and calliper
  colours), Body (street kit, widebody, wings, ride height), Engine, Chassis, Drive and Builds.
- Parts show on the car: GT wing or no wing, splitter, skirts and diffuser, a real widebody,
  slammed ride height, big brakes, bigger exhaust tips, a stripped cockpit.
- Ratings in the garage compare with stock; the Engine tab has a dyno chart.
- Up to three named builds per car; pick which one you race. Old saves carry over.

## 0.4.0 (unreleased): neon Japan, the Underground, better cars
- New look for every menu: neon Japan pixel style with turquoise frames, hot pink buttons,
  window title bars, scanlines and Japanese under the English.
- Track pick: a list on the left, the track flying past behind, its map and details on the
  right, Continue bottom right. Car pick: a list on the left, the car on a turntable in the
  garage, weather and time along the bottom right, Race bottom right.
- New track: Underground, Tokyo-style expressway tunnels with turquoise lights, Japanese
  road markings, direction boards, jet fans and exit signs.
- New car: 2017 Nissani GT-Ar (R35), four-wheel drive.
- All cars rebuilt from smooth curves after reference sheets: proper lamps, grilles, vents,
  mirrors and wheels with brake discs and callipers.
- Paint and glass reflect the lights round the car; black paint now shows its shape.
- The garage is a real room: a lock-up workshop with a turntable, light panels and a neon sign.
- Removed the car and weather tag from the driving screen.

## 0.3.0 (unreleased): feel, ratings, parts, a second car
- Feel panel (F4): drag sliders for power, brakes, grip, drift ease, lean, dive, squat, camera pull and shake while driving.
- Visible brake dive and launch squat, a nod on each upshift; the chase camera trails under power and closes in under braking.
- Car ratings: D to X class, a 1-1000 score, and Speed, Acceleration, Handling, Braking from 1 to 10, measured from the physics.
- Parts with trade-offs (engine, turbo, weight, tyres, brakes, suspension, gearing, four-wheel drive, wing, body kit). Fitting them in the garage comes next session.
- New car: 2009 Dodga Challenjer SRT8, with a V8 sound.
- Turbo whistle and flutter, gear changes you can hear, brake squeal on hard stops.
- Game controllers, in driving and menus.
- Removed the moths and dust round lamps; slower garage turntable.

## 0.2.0 (unreleased): iPad controls, menus, weather, effects
- iPad / phone touch controls: floating joystick, gas, brake, handbrake, gear buttons in
  manual, camera and pause. Size, opacity, sensitivity, dead zone, response, and a drag-to-place layout editor.
- New menus in a chunky pixel style (cyan and orange): main menu (Drive, Garage, Settings),
  track pick, race setup (weather and car), garage with stats and paint, pause (Resume,
  Restart, Settings, Main menu). The R reset key is gone; Restart is in the pause menu.
- Settings in four tabs (Gameplay, Graphics, Audio, Controls), with rebindable keys.
- The car is now the 1999 Nissani Skyliner GT-R (R34), modelled after the real one, with a
  proper cockpit: dashboard, working gauges, a steering wheel in front of the driver.
- Weather: Clear, Rain (about a quarter less grip) and Fog. Rain falls, the road goes wet and shiny.
- Effects you can switch off: tyre smoke, exhaust flames and pops, skid marks, sparks,
  moths, floating dust, headlight beams, lamp glow, wet reflections.
- Physics: body lean cut from 26° to about 3° and it never touches the road; lifting off
  now slows the car properly (100 to about 60 km/h in 8 s); the whole body outline
  collides with walls; fixed getting pinned against a wall and a sluggish pull-away at full lock.
- Fuller Starter Track: container yard, petrol station, power lines, a start gantry, corner
  chevrons, cones and bins, an infield of warehouses, a distant city glow. Far fewer draw calls.
- Low resolution by default.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-25): first drive
- Title screen with Computer / iPad choice, remembered on this device.
- Kaido R coupe built in code: flared arches, square bonnet, rear wing, four round tail lights, cockpit with moving steering wheel.
- Arcade physics: rear-wheel drive, wheelspin, handbrake drifts, weight transfer, steering/stability/drift assists.
- Automatic five-speed gearbox; brake at a standstill to reverse.
- Chase and cockpit cameras.
- Harbour Loop test road (1.9 km): streetlights, fog, barriers, fences, warehouses, trees, parked cars.
- Synthesised engine, tyre squeal, wind and impact sounds.
- Rev counter HUD, F3 debug panel, pause menu with graphics, volume and platform settings.
- Smoke test and driving bench.
