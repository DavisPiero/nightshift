# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-25): first drive
- Title screen with Computer / iPad choice, remembered on this device.
- Kaido R coupe built in code: flared arches, square bonnet, rear wing, four round tail lights, cockpit with moving steering wheel.
- Arcade physics: rear-wheel drive, wheelspin, handbrake drifts, weight transfer, steering/stability/drift assists.
- Automatic five-speed gearbox; brake at a standstill to reverse.
- Chase and cockpit cameras.
- Harbour Loop test road (1.9 km): streetlights, fog, barriers, fences, warehouses, trees, parked cars.
- Synthesised engine, tyre squeal, wind and impact sounds.
- Rev counter HUD, F3 debug panel, pause menu with graphics, volume and platform settings.
- Smoke test and driving bench.
