# NIGHTSHIFT: spec

## What makes it good
1. **In the car within seconds.** Title, pick Computer or iPad once, drive.
2. **Driving that forgives.** The car has weight and slides, but assists catch spins,
   countersteer for you a little and hold a drift once it starts. A kid with a keyboard
   should be able to drift on the first lap and never spin into a wall by accident.
3. **It sounds like a real engine.** Deep, layered, reacts to throttle, gear changes and
   lift-off. Never a mosquito.
4. **Night atmosphere.** Amber lamps, fog, dark greens, concrete, red tail lights.
   Restrained, like an obscure early-2000s console racer.
5. **A garage worth coming back to** (later): paint and upgrades that visibly and
   noticeably change the car.
6. **Cruising is allowed.** Timed laps are there, but you are never forced to race.

## Milestones (from the brief)
1. Foundation: loop, platform screen. **Done.**
2. Desktop driving: coupe, physics, keyboard, two cameras, debug, test road. **Done, signed off 2026-10-25.**
3. iPad controls: left joystick, right throttle/brake/handbrake, multitouch. **Next.**
4. Transmission and audio: manual mode option, better engine audio, tyre effects.
5. Night circuit: full track, scenery, lighting.
6. Racing: checkpoints, countdown, lap timing, results, restart.
7. Garage: workshop scene, orbit camera, paint, engine/tyre/suspension upgrades, one visual mod.
8. Saving and settings: assists, graphics, audio, iPad layout.
9. Polish and release.

## Not building
- Damage, fuel, tyre wear, or anything that punishes casual play.
- A simulator: no clutch pedal, no tyre temperatures, no setup sheets.
- Neon cyberpunk visuals or a modern glossy look.
- Real car brands, logos or exact copies of real cars.
- AI opponents before Milestone 6 is done (only the shared track data they will use).
- Online play.
